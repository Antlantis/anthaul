Anthaul Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Anthaul does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To let you continue a run and keep your records, Anthaul saves a few small files in its own storage on your device: your run in progress, the daily run, your unlocks and records, and your settings (sound, haptics and game speed). They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

What Anthaul does not do

  • No sign-in or account of any kind.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs in the game.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No in-app purchases.

TestFlight beta

If you test Anthaul through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect information such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own TestFlight terms and privacy policy. That information goes to Apple and to the developer through App Store Connect; Anthaul itself collects nothing.

Children

Because Anthaul collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, with a new effective date.
